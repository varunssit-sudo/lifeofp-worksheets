Life of P Worksheets

Privacy Policy

Life of P Worksheets (the website and the app). Last updated 26 November 2026.

In short

  • You do not need an account. We do not ask for a child's name, age, school or photo.
  • Worksheet answers, scores and child profiles stay on your own device. We never receive them.
  • We count visits without cookies and without storing personal data.
  • We only receive what you choose to send us in the request form.
  • We do not show ads and we do not sell or share data.

What stays on your device

When a child does a worksheet on the phone, their answers, scores and the first name or nickname you type for them are saved in the app's own storage on that device. This is what powers "My progress". It is never sent to us or anyone else. Clearing the app's data, or uninstalling the app, deletes it.

The app also keeps copies of the worksheets you have opened, so they work without the internet.

Visit counts

We use GoatCounter to count how many times pages are opened, so we know which topics are useful. GoatCounter does not use cookies, does not track people across sites and does not store IP addresses. We see totals such as "Fractions was opened 40 times this week", never who opened it.

The request form

Parents can ask for a new worksheet or video. If you use the form, we receive what you type (your name, an optional email address, the topic and details) and any photo, video or PDF you attach. We use it only to make the worksheet or video and, if you gave an email, to tell you it is ready. Please do not include a child's full name, school or face. Requests are kept in our private files and deleted on request.

The form is passed to us through Make.com, which acts only as our service provider for delivering the request.

YouTube videos

Lesson videos are on YouTube. Tapping a video link, a thumbnail or the QR code on a printed sheet opens YouTube, and YouTube's own privacy policy applies there. Video thumbnails shown in the app are loaded from YouTube's image servers.

Children

The worksheets are for children in Standard 2 (about 7 to 8 years old), used with a parent or teacher. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Children can do the worksheets without giving any personal information, and the progress feature stays on the device.

Your choices

  • Delete a child's progress in "My progress", or clear the app's data in your phone's settings.
  • To have a request you sent deleted, send a new request saying so, with the request ID you were shown.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. For any question about privacy, use the request form and write "Privacy" at the start of the details.
